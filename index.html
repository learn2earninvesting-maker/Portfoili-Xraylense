03

Quality & price check

Two simple questions for every stock: is it a good business, and is the price fair for the growth you're getting? Stocks are ranked by PEG — the price you pay for each unit of growth. Lower is better.
PEG under 1 = undervalued for its growth · 1–2 = fair · over 2 = expensive for the growth. PEG = P/E ÷ earnings growth. It fixes the flaw in P/E — a high P/E is fine if growth is high.

Promoters are a company's founders/owners. A high stake means their own money rides with yours; promoters adding is generally read as insider confidence, while promoters trimming or pledging shares is worth watching. This is context, not advice. Covered holdings only.

40-week EMA trend

The 40-week EMA (≈200-day) is a widely-used long-term trend line. A stock trading above it is generally in a longer-term uptrend; below it, a downtrend. It's a trend filter for context — not a buy or sell signal. Prices and the EMA are both from the coverage snapshot date.
